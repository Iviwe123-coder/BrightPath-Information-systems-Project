AI and Decision Support

Topic 3 · higher education analytics and decision support

BrightPath can use reporting and analytics to help staff understand enrolment, learning activity and student support needs. These tools organise evidence for a decision; they do not establish the full story about a student or remove the need for professional judgement.

Analytics maturity

Analytics can describe past results, explore possible explanations, predict who may need support, or suggest an action. For BrightPath, a pass-rate report is descriptive; an adviser check-in flag is predictive. A pattern does not prove its cause. (EDUCAUSE)

Decision types

Analytics can inform class-level follow-up, faculty planning or institution-wide decisions. Start with a clear question, check that the data is relevant and current, interpret results in context, and review whether the action helped. Staff remain responsible for decisions.

Higher education uses

A missed LMS login may indicate a need for support, but could also reflect offline study or connectivity limits. Treat a flag as a prompt for a conversation, explain what it means and allow corrections. Do not use it alone to restrict enrolment or learning access.

Risks and safeguards

Incomplete or biased records can make a model less reliable for some groups. Test performance, limit access to sensitive outputs, explain uncertainty and provide a way to challenge errors. A staff member should review consequential recommendations. UNESCO recommends human-centred, privacy-protective AI use in education. (UNESCO guidance)
