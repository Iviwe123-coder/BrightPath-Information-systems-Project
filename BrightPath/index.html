How higher education institutions manage information systems

Students, learning, finances and administration all depend on information systems. This resource explains how those systems work, where they go wrong, and what managers can do about it.

Every topic is explored through BrightPath Higher Education Institute, a fictional private institution in South Africa.

Topics

  • Data Management How institutions collect, store and look after student and operational data.
  • Privacy and POPIA What the Protection of Personal Information Act means for how personal information is handled.
  • AI and Decision Support How AI tools can support decisions in an institution, and where managers need to take care.
  • Systems Integration Why student, learning and finance systems fail to connect, and how to improve communication between them.
  • System Customisation Adapting systems to fit an institution's own processes.

The BrightPath scenario

BrightPath uses separate student management, learning management and finance systems. At the start of a semester, the systems disagree about who is in one module.

Staff spend two days comparing spreadsheets to find the differences. Meanwhile, an eligible student is excluded from a group assignment and an unregistered student gets access to the module. The Systems Integration page shows how to prevent this.
